# School mark sources

- `gakugei-hs.webp`: school emblem from the [Tokyo Gakugei University Senior High School tradition page](https://www.gakugei-hs.setagaya.tokyo.jp/tradition/), cropped and converted to WebP.
- `tuat-brand.png`: square TUAT mark from the [university's official social profile](https://x.com/TUAT_all), using the [current brand mark](https://www.tuat.ac.jp/outline/overview/symbol/mark/mark.html).
- `science-tokyo.svg`: Science S symbol from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Institute_of_Science_Tokyo_symbol.svg), sourced there from Science Tokyo.
- `tsinghua.webp`: university emblem from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tsinghua_University_Logo.svg), rendered to WebP.
